About GASFREE: the devices and screenshots

Mouse

  • Knobs, sliders: drag up / down (Shift: fine). Double-click: the default. The wheel over a knob turns it (Options can turn that off; Ctrl + wheel always turns it).
  • Hovering a knob lights the sockets that modulate it.
  • Pads: hold the button down and slide across them to play each one you pass over.
  • Cables: drag from a jack to another. Drag a cable off an input to move or remove it. Double-click or right-click a jack to unplug it.
  • Scrolling: the wheel off the knobs scrolls the rack (Shift + wheel: sideways); drag the rack's sides or the scrollbars. Zoom with − / + in the toolbar.
  • Devices: the buttons on a device's right side move it up / down or remove it; the chevron on its top left folds it.

Keyboard

  • Space: play / stop every synth. Tab: the rack's back (the audio and MIDI cables).
  • The device in the middle of the screen (a yellow line on its left) gets its buttons on the keys, the most used first, laid out as on the panel. Hold Shift to see which key is which; ` switches banks on devices with many buttons.
  • QWERTY (keys device): the letters play notes, Z / X change the octave.

MIDI

  • Choose the MIDI input in the keys device. Synths get their notes through MASTER MIDI or a cable on the back, on the channel set there.
  • MIDI learn (Options): click a knob, fader or button, then move a controller (or press a key / pad) to map it. The mappings are listed in Options, each with a button to remove it; they are saved with the rack.

Patches and sessions

  • Sessions save themselves in this browser as you play. Save as… keeps a patch; Export downloads it (Shift + Export: copies it to the clipboard); Import loads one.
  • Randomize gives every synth a random sound. Add device lists everything you can insert.

Synth Editor

  • ADD DEVICE › Custom › CUSTOM SYNTH: build your own synth. Design its FRONT PANEL (knobs, sliders, buttons, LEDs, jacks, lines, rectangles: drag them from the palette) and its BACK PANEL (audio sockets), then wire what is INSIDE (oscillators, filters, envelopes, LFOs, sequencers, effects). The ✎ on its left ear opens the editor again; Save to My synths keeps the design.

The song sequencer

  • The bar at the bottom: raise it with SEQUENCER. Tracks on MIDI channels, a piano roll (click to add a note, drag empty space to select notes, drag notes to move them, drag a note's end to lengthen the selection, right-click or Delete to remove). Each track's ⋮ menu copies, pastes, deletes and reorders it; double-click a name to rename it. LOAD MIDI FILE replaces the song with a .mid file. ⇱ WINDOW detaches it into its own window (closing it brings it back). CV tracks: knobs turned while recording (REC, playing) are recorded and played back; + CV makes one for the next knob touched; EXPORT WAV records the song played once (in real time) as a WAV file, REC from the keys, LOOP, BPM, BARS, GRID, SYNC (MIDI clock).
